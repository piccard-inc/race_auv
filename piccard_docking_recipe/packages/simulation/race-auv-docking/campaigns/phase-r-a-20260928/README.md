# Phase R-A campaign (M2), 2026-09-28

The pose missions of the M2 preregistration, "Phase R-A v0.1" on piccard-experiments #87, as amended by v0.2 the
same day. Tracking: piccard-inc/piccard-physical-ai#96. Simulation only. Ground truth scores and never navigates.
The planner commands through MVP `bhv_direct_control` only.

The files in `missions/` are generated by `tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --write-missions` and must equal
their definitions there (a test checks this). Each is a `piccard.race-auv.pose-mission/v1` mission that
`runtime/mission.py` validates.

## Pose derivation

The derivation is the same as M1 (`examples/m1-smoke-request.json`, `context.pose_derivation`). The mission schema
has no field for it, so each job's context carries it as `pose_derivation`.

- The station dock point is at Stonefish world (3.59, 0.325, 3.91).
- For a stand-off `s`, the AUV dock point is the station dock point minus `s` along x_W.
- For a level AUV heading north, cg_link sits 0.395 m behind the dock point and 0.13 m above it.
- The controller's world_ned is `x_C = -y_W`, `y_C = x_W + 3.8`, `yaw_C = yaw_W + 1.571`.

So each pose commands cg_link at:
- `x_C = -0.325 + lateral`
- `y_C = 6.995 - s`
- `z_C = 3.78 + depth` (z_C points down: +0.5 is deeper)
- `yaw_C = 1.571 + yaw`

A yaw step turns about cg_link, the frame the controller regulates, so it moves the nose dock point about 0.12 m
sideways. This is reported, not corrected.

## Missions

Every mission starts with M1's 40 s dive: cg_link at (-0.325, 0.0, 3.78), 7 m in front of the station. The dive
does not reach depth in 40 s, so the first approach pose finishes it. Apart from `1step`, every mission then runs
the staged approach: 3.0 m for 120 s, then 1.5 m for 120 s.

| File | Key | Poses after the dive (stand-off m, dwell s) | Dwell s | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `m-approach-1step-v1.json` | `1step` | approach 1.5 (200); hold_1p5m (60). M1's poses exactly. | 300 | reference: R-A0 pairs, staged vs single step |
| `m-approach-staged-v1.json` | `staged` | approach_3m 3.0 (120); approach_1p5m 1.5 (120); hold_1p5m (60) | 340 | R-A1 probes and selection; R-A2 |
| `m-lateral-yaw-v1.json` | `lateral` | staged; lateral_plus x_C +0.5, lateral_minus -0.5, lateral_center, yaw_plus +0.3 rad, yaw_minus -0.3 rad, return (90 each); hold_1p5m (60) | 880 | holdout, never used for selection |

## Stages

```bash
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage a0 --out a0.json        # 8: 3 display pairs + 1 onboard pair, 1step, N
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage a1 --out a1.json        # 9: N, 6 probes, N repeat on staged; N on 1step
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage holdout --selected <knob> --out holdout.json   # 4
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage a2 --out a2.json        # 2: lab_configured vs black_square_edge tag size
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage b --selected <knob> --out b.json               # 2
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage a1 --rerun pr-a1-p10-staged-r1 --out rerun.json # the one re-run
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage a0b --selected p10 --out a0b.json  # 12 (#102): 6 staged display pairs
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage holdout2 --selected p10 --out ho2.json  # 2 (#102): N and p10 on depth2
python3 tools/campaigns/build_phase_r_campaign.py --stage a0c --selected p10 --out a0c.json  # 2 (#102): onboard A/B pair
```

**Stage a0c** (#102, closes #86's remaining scope). It is for the next RACE pin: the M2 pin `9f06b6c5` predates
the onboard recorder.
- One `M-approach-staged` pair at the selected gains: `{display_video, onboard_camera_video}` against no media.
- Roles `isolation_onboard_without_video` / `isolation_onboard_with_video`.
- Two checks compare the pair:
  - `recording_isolation_check.py --min-pairs 1` (settling, timing, minimum distance);
  - `race_onboard_load_check.py` (cam_front / cam_down image delivery).

**Floor clearance** (#102). Every stage build checks every pose of its missions, and fails if the vehicle would be
closer than 0.3 m to the tank floor. The CLI prints each mission's minimum clearance to stderr.
- **Clearance:** the smallest floor height under the vehicle's footprint, widened by 0.3 m for tracking and
  drift, minus the depth of the vehicle's lowest point (level, at the commanded pose).
- **Geometry:** `campaigns/tank-floor-v1.json`, written by `tools/campaigns/extract_tank_floor.py` from the
  pinned world_of_stonefish (`d51d59e`), with every source file's sha256. It holds the interior floor (world
  z 4.487–4.494) and the vehicle's lowest point, 0.180 m below the Base (the legs).
- **Check against M2:** the floor under the approach corridor minus 0.180 m gives 4.310 m, where both M2 holdout
  depth runs rested (4.309 m).
- **Results:**

  | Pose | Clearance |
  |---|---|
  | M-depth-hold v1 `depth_plus` | 0.028 m, which is why it rested on the floor |
  | v2 `depth_plus` | 0.328 m |
  | Every other pose | at least 0.527 m |

- **v1:** `depth` is kept for the record. Only its own stage (holdout) may rebuild it.

The tank mesh is a double shell. The vehicle swims in the inner box (x_W ±4.57, y_W ±3.05, floor about 4.49).
`runtime/mission.py`'s bounds (x_C ±3.66, y_C −1.38 to 8.98, z up to 5.10) are the **outer** shell's, so they admit
poses outside the inner walls or below the floor. The builder's check covers the missions built here; the runtime is
unchanged.

**Stage a0b** (#102). R-A0's three recorded runs all came within the station's proximity line and the unrecorded
ones didn't, so a0b asks whether that is chance or a directional effect:
- Six display-video pairs on `M-approach-staged` at the selected gains.
- The order alternates within pairs: no-video, video, video, no-video, and so on.
- Roles `isolation_display_without_video` / `isolation_display_with_video`, repeats 1–6.
- `recording_isolation_check.py` judges them, now including a two-sided sign test on the per-pair change in
  minimum distance.

**Jobs file.** The output is `{recipe, horizon_s, wall_timeout_s, jobs: [{trial_id, gains, mission, context,
media?}]}`, and the same inputs give the same bytes.

**Horizon and timeout.** There is one horizon per jobs file: the stage's longest mission dwell plus 30 s.
- a0: 330 s
- a1: 370 s
- holdout: 910 s
- a2: 370 s
- b: 550 s

The wall timeout is the horizon plus 150 s. Trials stop at `mission_complete`, so a shorter mission doesn't wait
out the horizon.

**Gains.** Only docking x and y change, and always both together, from the M1 nominal N
(p 5, i 0.2, d 0, v 5, ±15). The probes are p 2.5, p 10, i 0, i 0.5, v 10 and v 20.

**Media.** Only the R-A0 pairs have media.

**Trial ids.** They take the form `pr-<a0|a1|ho|a2|b>-<knob>-<mission>[-<variant>]-r<n>`.

**Context.** Each context (`piccard.race-auv.phase-r-a-context/v1`) records:
- the trial id and campaign id;
- the protocol and stage;
- the gain set and its docking x/y overrides;
- the mission key and its sha256. This is the hash of the committed file's bytes. trial.json's `mission_sha256`
  differs, because the submit path re-renders integral floats as integers (`0.0` as `0`). The content is the same,
  and `race_m2_metrics.py` accepts either form and reports which one matched;
- the role and repeat;
- the pose derivation.

**Re-run.** A re-run keeps the stage's horizon and adds `rerun_of`.

Budget: about 10 420 s of dwell over 25 trials, about 3.5 h with startup (amendment v0.2).

## Analysis

- `tools/analysis/race_m2_metrics.py` computes the fixed metrics per trial, and the R-A1 selection rule with
  `--matrix`.
- For R-A0, `tools/analysis/recording_isolation_check.py` handles the display pairs, with RACE settling under the
  v0.2 definition. `tools/analysis/race_onboard_load_check.py` handles the onboard pair.
